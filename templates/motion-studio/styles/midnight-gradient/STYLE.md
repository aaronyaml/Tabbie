# 11 · Midnight Gradient

Tokens: `style.json`. Craft that applies to every style: `MOTION_RULES.md`. Reference evidence: ref10 in
`styles/_research/REFERENCES.md` (a 43 s social product promo, 7 shots). Principles only; no frame is copied.

**Idea:** a premium social promo on a near-black stage. The type does the work: one word lands at a time,
assembled from letters scattered across the frame, outline words fill, and one hero word ignites in a hot pink →
orange → violet gradient with a dark echo sliding behind it and confetti dots popping. Product moments are glowing,
rounded UI: an input pill whose button pulses gradient rings, round gradient feature icons, dark list cards.

## Visual grammar & composition
- One word or one UI element centred on black, with a lot of space around it.
- A small persistent mark in a corner (the product's logo and name or handle) anchors the frame.
- Full width only for the hero word and its echo, and for one white flash page (a loose photo collage) at most.
- Thin dashed guides (a circle, a box) can appear around outline words as they fill.

## Typography
- Inter Tight 800 for words, lower case, tight tracking (−0.035 em); Inter 400/600 for UI text and labels.
- One to three words on screen at a time. One hero word per film gets the gradient fill.

## Colour & material
- Near-black `#050208` with a soft lift `#17131C` behind the centre, white `#F6F3F1`.
- Gradient family only: orange `#FF8A3D` → pink `#FF3D74` → violet `#7B3DFF`, teal `#2EC9B8` as a dot accent.
- Colour lives in gradients (hero word, icons, pill border, rings) and confetti (small squares and dots), never in
  flat panels. Glows are soft and come from the content.

## Animation curves & timing
- On ones. Arrivals are expo-out (`enter` [0.16,1,0.3,1]): fast launch, long settle. Exits accelerate away.
- Letters spring into place with a 1–2 f stagger; confetti and icons pop with a short overshoot.
- One word per beat (8–20 f). UI moments hold 1–2 s so they can be read.

## Transition vocabulary (the seven techniques, this style's way)
- **Typographic — letter assembly** (signature): letters fly from scattered spots to form the word, and scatter back.
- **Typographic — gradient ignition** (signature): a white or outline word fills with the gradient left → right
  while a dark echo slides behind and confetti pops.
- **Seamless zoom — ring pulse** (signature): gradient rings expand from a glowing control past the frame; the next
  scene is inside the last ring.
- **Object morph — pill morph:** the pill shrinks and splits into round gradient icons, or icons merge into a pill.
- **Motion continuity — list scroll:** dark cards stream upward; the next word rides the same motion.
- **Flash — flash page:** one hard cut to a white page, then a black circle irises back to the dark stage.
- **Match cut:** the glowing button becomes the app icon in the reveal.

## Camera
Mostly locked, with slow drifts and pushes. Lists scroll, the pill slides in from the edge. Pushes happen through
the ring pulse rather than a camera move.

## Lighting & texture
Emissive light only: soft glows behind gradients, a warm lens-flare streak inside a glass tile, faint bokeh. Very
light grain (≈ 3 %).

## Sound
A modern pop/electronic bed with a steady beat. Whooshes under letter flights, pops and sparkles for confetti, a
soft swell for ring pulses, a deep hit on the gradient word, a bright hit on the flash page.

## Do / don't
- Do: one word at a time, one gradient hero word, glowing rounded UI, a corner mark, lots of black.
- Don't: flat primary colours, rainbow gradients, sentences on screen, cuts on every beat, paper or grain looks.

## Motion grammar
- **Pacing:** a word per beat inside long shots, 1–2 cuts per 10 s, UI moments held 1–2 s.
- **Rhythm:** beat-driven words, syncopated confetti, rings on the downbeat.
- **Easing:** expo-out arrivals, accelerating exits, soft springs for letters and pops.
- **Movement:** letter flights, outline fills, gradient sweeps, ring pulses, list scrolls, icon pops, echo slides,
  flare blooms.
- **Density:** one element at a time; sparse confetti around hero moments.

## Scene archetypes
- **A · Word beat:** one short word centred on black; letters assemble, outline fills, letters scatter.
- **B · Glow pill:** an input pill slides in, text types, the gradient button glows and rings pulse out.
- **C · Feature row:** two to four round gradient icons with labels pop in, confetti around.
- **D · Gradient hero:** the punchline word ignites in the gradient with a dark echo and confetti.
- **E · App reveal:** a flare streak blooms in a glass tile and resolves to the app icon with its name.

## Sequence model
Opening (15 %, E then A) → letter assembly → development (30 %, A/B/C) → ring pulse → escalation (25 %, A/C, list
scroll, one flash page) → flash → reveal (15 %, D) → letter assembly → resolution (15 %, E). 10–45 s.

**Example mini-edit (15 s AI creator app ad):** 1 · a flare blooms in a glass tile, pink squares drift (0–60) →
2 · "one app" assembles as a dashed outline and fills white (60–120) → ring pulse → 3 · the pill slides in, "make a
lo-fi beat" types, rings pulse from the button (120–210) → pill morph → 4 · three gradient icons: music, speech,
images (210–285) → 5 · "without" in outline, then "limits" ignites with an echo (285–375) → 6 · the letters gather
into the app icon and the name types below (375–450).

## Element behaviour
- **Text:** one word at a time, lower case, white; one gradient hero word. **Logo:** small in a corner, full size in
  the reveal. **Icons:** round gradient badges with white line glyphs. **Images:** only on the flash page.
  **UI:** dark and rounded (pill, list cards, a phone screen), in the film's colours. **Shapes:** pills, circles,
  tiles, dashed guides. **Background:** near-black. **Particles:** small squares and dots in the gradient colours.

## Audio relationship
Beat-driven and syncopated. Each word lands on a beat, confetti on off-beats, ring pulses on the downbeat, the hero
word on the strongest hit. SFX: whooshes, pops, sparkles, a swell, a deep hit, a bright flash hit.
